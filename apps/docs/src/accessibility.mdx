# Accessibility conformance

Target: WCAG 2.1 level AA. This page says, component by component, what the library does and how each claim is checked. It is a statement of what was verified, not a certification: nobody outside the project has audited it, and no real screen reader (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver) has been used by the agents that wrote and tested the code. Testing with screen readers is a recommended manual step for the maintainer.

## How things are verified

- **Unit test**: Vitest and Testing Library in jsdom, including `axe` (`vitest-axe`) and keyboard tests with `userEvent`. Run with `pnpm test`.
- **Story with `play`**: interaction tests with `userEvent` from `@storybook/test`, run in Chromium by the Storybook test runner.
- **axe in Chromium**: `pnpm test:a11y` runs axe (rules `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21a`, `wcag21aa`) on every story. Components have a `DarkTheme` story, so both themes are covered.
- **Reflow**: `pnpm test:a11y:reflow` visits every story in a 320px wide viewport and fails if the page scrolls horizontally.
- **Sizes**: `pnpm test:a11y:sizes` measures rendered sizes in Chromium (see "Sizes and targets").
- **Manual**: checked by a person. For this audit nothing is claimed as manual unless the line says so.

## Reflow (WCAG 1.4.10)

`pnpm test:a11y:reflow` runs the test runner with a viewport 320px wide and fails if the page of any story scrolls horizontally. Tables scroll inside their own container (`Table/HorizontalScroll`), so the page does not scroll and the story passes without an exception. The stories `LongWord` of `Card`, `Accordion` and `Dialog` (DS-053, DS-057) pass too.

Exceptions: none.

## Motion (WCAG 2.3.3)

`styles/motion.css` sets the duration of every animation and transition to zero under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, for every element with a `ds-` class and its `::before` and `::after`. A unit test (`motion.test.ts`) reads all component CSS files and fails if a rule with `animation` or `transition` does not select such an element.

## Sizes and targets (WCAG 2.5.8)

`pnpm test:a11y:sizes` runs in Chromium with a base font size of 16px:

- On the story `Accessibility/Sizes` it measures the rendered height of `Button`, `IconButton` (also the width), `Input`, the `Select` trigger and a `Textarea` with `rows` of 1 in every size, and fails below 32px for `sm`, 40px for `md` and 48px for `lg`. The message names the component and the size.
- On every story it measures every interactive element and fails if one is smaller than 24 by 24px.

Exceptions allowed by the criterion and by the check:

- Links inside a sentence (`display: inline`) are not measured (the inline exception of WCAG 2.5.8).
- Elements hidden from assistive technology (`aria-hidden`, `hidden`, `inert`, `display: none`) are not measured, such as the native inputs Radix keeps for forms.
- A control inside a `label` (`Checkbox`, `RadioGroup`, `Switch`) is measured through its label, and the text field of `Input` through its wrapper, because pressing anywhere on them activates the control.

The sort button of the table header was 20px tall and now has a minimum height of 24px.

## Visual review of the DataTable

Checked in Chromium, in the light and dark themes, at 1280px (`pnpm test:a11y`) and at 320px (`pnpm test:a11y:reflow`), for the stories `Default`, `Empty`, `NoPagination`, `KeyboardInteraction`, `LiveSummary` and `DarkTheme` (and the other `DataTable` stories):

- The footer (summary and `Pagination`) stays inside the component and its two parts do not overlap (measured).
- When the page changes, the `aria-live="polite"` element changes text (`play` of `LiveSummary`).
- A focus ring is drawn on the sortable headers and the `Pagination` buttons (checked with the keyboard, `:focus-visible` and the computed outline).
- axe finds no contrast problem in the header and the rows, including `striped`, in both themes.

With `pageSize` of 0 there is no summary. That is accepted behaviour, not a defect.

These checks measure geometry and styles; they do not replace looking at the page.

## Known limitations

- No test with a real screen reader. Names, roles and live regions are checked with axe and Testing Library, which do not show how each screen reader announces them.
- Windows high contrast mode (`forced-colors`) is not handled.
- WCAG 2.2 and level AAA are out of scope.
